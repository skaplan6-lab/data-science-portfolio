What is Data Science to Me?

I think learning data science in social contexts is important because data can help us understand patterns in people and their behavior. Since I also study psychology, I am interested in seeing how data can be used to answer questions about people and real-world issues. At the same time, I think using data about people comes with responsibilities, especially making sure the data is used fairly and people's privacy is respected. Through this course, I hope to better understand how data science can be used to study people in a responsible and meaningful way.

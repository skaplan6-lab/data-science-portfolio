What is Data Science to Me?

I think learning about data in social contexts is important because data can help us better understand people and real-world issues. Since I study both data science and psychology, I am especially interested in how data can be used to understand behavior and find patterns in how people think, work, and interact. When using data on real people, it's important to protect their privacy and collect and use data in an ethical way. I think this is important when seeing how it affects personal peoples lives.
